\section{Configuration Decisions}
Patient access is restricted to the responsible physician. Clinical decisions require approval of the exact saved revision; pending drafts are excluded from accepted memory. Before evaluation, select permitted cases, analytical models, evidence sources and orchestration responsibilities. Record the case count, reference process, rubric, workload and thresholds with each comparison configuration.
