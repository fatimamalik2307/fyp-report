Zeest brings patient history, consultation notes and clinical findings into one physician workspace. Each physician accesses their own patient records, selects an encounter and reviews the history. Consultation audio becomes editable structured notes, while supported imaging, laboratory information and medication context contribute separate findings. Retrieved clinical guidelines accompany a diagnosis and treatment draft, and critic findings help the physician inspect its support and limitations. Approval records the reviewed revision as a clinical decision; modification retains a pending draft, and rejection preserves the review action without accepting the content. Longitudinal memory connects authorised source information and approved decisions across encounters. The current contribution comprises requirements, interface designs, data relationships, architecture and an evaluation method comparing simpler variants with the full system. Implementation and measured comparisons remain future work. Clinical coverage depends on permitted de-identified or synthetic data, suitable existing models and selected knowledge resources, with physician judgement governing every accepted decision.
